"Get started" is not « Commencer ». It looks like a faithful translation — it's even grammatically perfect — but it doesn't do the same job.

## CTAs are speech acts, not labels

An English CTA is an invitation. A French CTA carries register, politeness and expectation. When you translate the words but not the intent, you get buttons that feel bossy, vague or oddly formal.

## Three quick wins

1. **Test the infinitive vs. the imperative.** French UI convention often prefers the infinitive (« Créer un compte ») where English uses an imperative.
2. **Watch the urgency register.** "Don't miss out!" translated literally reads as aggressive in French.
3. **Shorten after translating, not before.** French runs 15–25% longer than English; budget for it in your button widths.
