Generative Engine Optimization changes what "translating your content for search" means — and most localization briefs haven't caught up.

## From keywords to answers

Classic SEO translation optimized for what French users *type*. GEO optimizes for what French users *ask* — full questions, in natural language, to AI assistants that synthesize answers from your content.

## What this changes for your French content

- Structure content around **questions and direct answers**, not keyword density.
- Keep terminology **consistent and citable** — assistants reward clarity.
- Localize your **entities** (product names, feature names) so they're recognizable across sources.
